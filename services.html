Annuities & Retirement Income Planning

Objective fiduciary guidance across multi-year fixed guarantees, index cap accumulation, immediate pension alternatives, and tax-advantaged legacy structures.

ANNUITY
Best For: Conservative Savers (CD Alternative)

Fixed Annuities (MYGA)

Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuities lock in an ironclad annual yield (typically 5.2% - 5.8%) for 3 to 10 years with 100% tax-deferred compounding and zero stock market exposure.

  • Guaranteed fixed compounding rate
  • 100% principal insulation from crashes
  • 10% annual penalty-free liquidity access
ANNUITY
Best For: Growth Investors Wanting Downside Buffers

Variable & Buffered Annuities

Structured Registered Index-Linked Annuities (RILAs) provide higher growth ceilings with institutional downside loss buffers (e.g., insurer absorbs the first 10% - 20% of market drops).

  • High equity participation rates (S&P/Nasdaq)
  • 10% to 20% downside barrier or buffer
  • Tax-deferred rebalancing between subaccounts
ANNUITY
Best For: Pre-Retirees Wanting Growth with 0% Floor

Fixed Indexed Annuities (FIA)

Our flagship solution. Crediting is linked to benchmark indices (S&P 500, MSCI) up to a cap (e.g. 6.5% - 9.0%), but backed by an unbreakable 0% floor—losses are mathematically impossible.

  • 0% Floor Guarantee: Never lose principal
  • Annual lock-in ratchet: Gains become permanent
  • Optional Guaranteed Lifetime Withdrawal Rider
ANNUITY
Best For: Immediate Monthly Retirement Paychecks

Immediate Annuities (SPIA)

Single Premium Immediate Annuities convert a lump sum into guaranteed monthly direct deposits starting within 30 days. Perfect substitute for a vanishing company pension.

  • Income starts immediately (month 1)
  • Joint-and-survivor options for married couples
  • Exclusion ratio tax benefits on non-qualified funds
ANNUITY
Best For: Longevity Insurance & QLAC Tax Shields

Deferred Longevity (DIA / QLAC)

Commit funds today to turn on massive guaranteed payments decades later (ages 75–85). Qualified Longevity Annuity Contracts (QLACs) also defer IRS Required Minimum Distributions.

  • Massive payout rate due to mortality pooling
  • Defers RMD taxes up to $200,000 to age 85
  • Solves the "what if I live to 98" risk completely
ANNUITY
Best For: Maintaining Account Control While Drawing Guaranteed Income

Lifetime Income Riders (GLWB)

Guaranteed Lifetime Withdrawal Benefits allow you to take lifetime income without annuitizing (surrendering control of your account balance). If you pass away, remaining funds go to heirs.

  • Retain account balance and principal ownership
  • Roll-up bonus rates (often 7% - 10% during deferral)
  • Continued spousal checks after primary death

Legacy & Beneficiary Design

PROTECTION
Best For: Shielding Retirement Wealth from Nursing Costs

Asset-Based Long-Term Care

Avoid "use-it-or-lose-it" traditional policies. Hybrid annuity/LTC contracts multiply your benefit 2x to 3x for healthcare, but return 100% of unused principal to heirs if care is never needed.

  • 100% principal return if care is never required
  • 200% to 300% tax-free healthcare benefit pool
  • Protection against Medicaid spend-down rules
